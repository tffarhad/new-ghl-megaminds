The worst failures in GoHighLevel are the quiet ones. Nothing errors, nothing alerts you, the money just stops.

## What we look for first

1. A workflow toggled off by accident months ago.
2. An expired or disconnected phone number.
3. A form that stopped mapping to the pipeline.
4. Email sending paused after a bounce spike.
5. A calendar with no availability left on it.
6. A payment integration that quietly disconnected.

None of these throw an error. That is exactly why a monthly health check pays for itself — someone is actually looking.
