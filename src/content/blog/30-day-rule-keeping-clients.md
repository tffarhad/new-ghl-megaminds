A client who never logs in is a client who will cancel. The first 30 days decide almost everything.

## What the first month should look like

- **Day 1:** a real welcome call, not just an email.
- **Week 1:** their account set up *for* them so they see value before they touch anything.
- **Weeks 2–4:** a short check in and one training call.

The goal is a single moment where they think *oh, this actually works*. Get that moment inside 30 days and they stay. Miss it and no discount will save the account.
