Most resellers pick a price out of thin air, then panic when the bill arrives. You do not need to guess.

## Start from the floor

1. Add up what one client costs you a month: your share of the HighLevel plan plus their expected usage.
2. That number is your **floor** — never sell below it.
3. Add the profit you want per client on top.
4. Sanity check against what people already pay for the tools you are replacing.

Build three plans around that number. Most buyers pick the middle one, so make the middle one the offer you actually want to sell.
