SaaS Mode looks simple in the demo. Flip it on, set a price, sell. Then the first HighLevel bill lands and it is bigger than the money coming in.

## The leak is almost always rebilling

When rebilling is off, **you** pay for every text, call, email, and AI credit your clients burn. They use the product you sold them, and the usage lands on your card.

Turn rebilling on, set a sensible markup, and connect Stripe properly. Do that before you take a single payment. If your SaaS is already live and leaking, a free leak check will show you exactly what each switch is costing you a month.
