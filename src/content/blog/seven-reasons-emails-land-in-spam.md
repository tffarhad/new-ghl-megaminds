If your emails are landing in spam, it is almost never bad luck. It is usually one of these.

## The usual suspects

1. No SPF, DKIM, or DMARC records on your domain.
2. Sending from a free address like gmail.com instead of your domain.
3. A brand new domain with no sending history.
4. Big image, tiny text emails that look like ads.
5. Spam trigger words in the subject line.
6. No easy unsubscribe link.
7. Emailing people who never asked to hear from you.

Fix the first three today and most inbox problems disappear.
