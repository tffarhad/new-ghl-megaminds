A customer calls while you are busy. You miss it. Normally that person just calls the next business. Missed call text back closes that gap automatically.

## How it works

The moment a call goes unanswered, the system sends a text from your number:

> Sorry we missed you! This is [Business]. What can we help with?

That is it. The lead replies by text, you answer when you can, and the job stays yours. It is ten minutes to set up and it is usually the first thing we turn on for a new client.
