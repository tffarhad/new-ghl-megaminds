A2P registration blocks more launches than anything else in GoHighLevel, and almost always because the form is filled in vaguely.

## Pass it the first time

- Use a real, registered business name and matching website.
- Describe your use case in plain, specific words: what you send, to whom, and why.
- Show a sample message that includes your business name and an opt out.
- Make sure your website has a visible privacy policy.

Carriers are not trying to trip you up. They just want to see a real business sending messages people asked for. Show them that clearly and approval is quick.
